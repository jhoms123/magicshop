# Third-party asset notices

This starter bundles existing artwork and a font. No pixel-art images were generated for this project. Original pixel pixels were retained; the Kenney tiles in the atlases were repacked from individual source tiles for simpler runtime slicing.

- Packs: [16x16 Puny World Tileset](https://opengameart.org/content/16x16-puny-world-tileset) and [Puny Characters](https://opengameart.org/content/puny-characters).
- License: CC0 1.0. The OpenGameArt pages explicitly permit personal and commercial use without attribution.
- Bundled files: `Assets/Resources/Art/PunyWorld.png`, `PunyPlayer.png`, and `PunyMerchant.png`.
- The source character sheets are the Puny `player.png` and `vela.png` spritesheets. Their original 32x32 frames are used for the player and traveling merchant animations.
- SHA-256 for the unmodified Puny World sheet: `14d285bde8dee96cb0113947c8cce4922b86bf1d033b19123112145c7cef3a38`.
