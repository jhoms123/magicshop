# Third-party asset notices

This starter bundles selected artwork and a font from the sources below. The witch sampler's creator discloses that its graphics were made with AI assistance. Sprites used by the world are cropped or arranged into small purpose-built atlases; the full Sprout Lands asset sheets are not included.

- Artist: Cup Nooble.
- Pack: [Sprout Lands Asset Pack](https://cupnooble.itch.io/sprout-lands-asset-pack).
- Reference project: [2D Farming Game](https://github.com/gadget-hq/2d-farming-game), which includes the Basic pack and its `Assets/Sprout Lands/read_me.txt` license text.
- Bundled license text: [read_me.txt](https://github.com/gadget-hq/2d-farming-game/blob/main/Assets/Sprout%20Lands/read_me.txt).
- Derived sprites in `Assets/Resources/Art/MagicFarm/`: `Ground.png` (grass and tilled earth), `Nature.png` (trees, flowers and rocks), `Crops.png` (mushroom, carrot and berry growth stages), `People.png` (four facing directions with two walk frames), and `Furniture.png` (chest, table, stool and fence).
- The included source is the free Basic pack. Its license permits modification and use in non-commercial projects, requires credit, and allows open-source projects when they include a note with the credit and license terms. It does not permit redistributing the asset pack itself. Commercial projects require a separate commercial license; none is included here.
